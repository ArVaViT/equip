# Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · in force from 13 August 2026**

Equip is a platform for Bible schools. This document sets out what the platform
stores, why, who can see it, and how to take it away or delete it. It is written
for a person rather than a lawyer, and it is deliberately short: a policy that
cannot be read in five minutes does not get read.

## Who we are

Equip is a non-commercial project, not a company. There are people behind the
platform rather than a legal entity, and questions go to the address in the site
footer. We say this plainly because it is worth knowing before you sign up: in a
dispute there is nobody to escalate to except us.

## What we store

**What you enter yourself.** Name, email address, profile photograph, preferred
language. Your name is printed on certificates and grade sheets, so it should be
the name you want to be recorded under.

**What you do on a course.** Chapters read, quiz answers, work submitted, marks
and teacher feedback, certificates earned, and the declarations you make about
your own work.

**Technical records.** Sign-in times, the IP address at the moment you accept
this document and at the moment you hand in work, and application error reports.
The IP is stored precisely so that an acceptance and a declaration can be shown
to have actually been made — not to track anyone, and it is used for nothing
else.

We do **not** store payment details: there are no payments on the platform.

## Who can see it

- **Your course teacher** — your work, answers, marks and progress.
- **Your school's administrator** — the same, plus student lists and grade sheets.
- **Other students** — only your name and photograph, and only where that is part
  of the coursework itself (a review you left on a course, for instance).
- **Nobody outside the platform.** We do not sell data, hand it to advertisers, or
  share it with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers below.

## The providers the platform cannot run without

| Who | What they see | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | the database and account sign-in | storing everything above |
| Vercel | requests to the site | delivering the application |
| Datadog | technical logs and errors | so we notice breakage before you do |

Each of them processes data on our instructions; none of them owns it.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When an account is deleted the coursework goes
with it — results, submissions, certificates — with one exception: **an issued
certificate, and the grade sheet recording it, remain**. A school cannot
retroactively un-witness what it has already witnessed, which is exactly why
those documents are frozen as a snapshot at the moment they are issued.

## What you can do

- See and change your data — in your profile.
- Get a copy of everything held about you — write to us.
- Delete your account — in your profile, subject to the certificate note above.
- Withdraw consent — which means deleting the account: using the platform without
  agreeing to this document is not technically possible.

## Age

You can register yourself from **16**. A younger student is enrolled by a school
administrator, and in that case parental consent is the school's responsibility —
they know the family and we do not.

## Changes

If this document changes materially we ask you to agree again, and we record
which version you agreed to. Old versions are not rewritten. Your consent is a
record of a specific text, not a tick in the abstract.

## Law

The platform is operated from the United States (Indiana), and the law of that
state applies to this document.
