# Terms of Use

**Version 1.0 · in force from 13 August 2026**

## What this platform is

Equip is a tool for Bible schools: a school builds courses, teachers mark work,
students study and earn certificates. We provide the software. The teaching
content, the marks, and the decision to award a certificate belong to the school.

## Your account

One person, one account, under a real name. That is not a formality: your name is
printed on the certificate and the grade sheet, and a certificate in an invented
name is worth nothing. Your password is your responsibility; if you think someone
else has used it, change it and tell us.

You can register yourself from 16. Younger than that, through a school
administrator.

## Your work is yours

Everything you write in an assignment stays yours. We store it, show it to your
teacher, and use it for nothing else: we do not publish it, train models on it,
or pass it on.

## Academic integrity

Every submission carries a declaration: whether the work is your own, and whether
AI was used. The rule about AI is set by the course, and it is on the screen at
the moment you hand in.

We do not run submissions through AI detectors and do not intend to. Those
detectors wrongly accuse writers working in a second language — most of this
school — and miss lightly edited text. An accusation that cannot be supported is
worse than no accusation at all.

An honestly declared breach of the rule is a confession, not a forgery: the
platform will never refuse work for ticking "AI was used". What follows is the
teacher's decision.

## What you may not do

- Pass off someone else's work as your own.
- Post material that abuses people or incites hatred.
- Try to reach other people's accounts or data.
- Use the platform to send advertising.

A school may remove a student from its own course. We may close an account used
for any of the above — and we will say why.

## Teachers and schools

Teaching rights are granted by a school administrator, not by the platform.
Whoever awards marks and signs a grade sheet answers for what they signed. A mark
entered in the system is an act by a specific person, and the platform's log
records who performed it and when.

## Certificates

A certificate is issued by the school, not by Equip. We record the moment of
issue and print the document so that it cannot be quietly rewritten afterwards:
if the school changes its name in March, a certificate issued in February stays
as it was issued.

An Equip certificate is not a state-recognised educational qualification.

## What we do not promise

The platform is provided as it is. We do not guarantee it will never break, and
we are not liable for loss caused by it being unavailable. We take backups, but
the only copy of work that matters to you should not live only with us.

## Changes

Material changes come with a fresh acceptance, and with a record of which version
you accepted.
