## Your own work stays yours

Everything you write in an assignment stays yours. We store it, show it to your

## Academic integrity

Every submission carries a declaration: whether the work is your own, and
whether AI was used. The rule about AI is set by the course, and it is on the
screen at the moment you hand in.

We do not run submissions through AI detectors and do not intend to. Those
detectors wrongly accuse writers working in a second language — most of this
school — and miss lightly edited text. An accusation that cannot be supported
is worse than no accusation at all.

An honestly declared breach of the rule is a confession, not a forgery: the
platform will never refuse work for ticking "AI was used". What follows is the
teacher's decision.

## Certificates

A certificate is issued by the school, not by Equip. We record the moment of
issue and print the document so that it cannot be quietly rewritten afterwards:
if the school changes its name in March, a certificate issued in February stays
as it was issued.

An Equip certificate is **evidence that a course was completed**. It is not a
state-recognised educational qualification, not a diploma, not a licence to
minister, and Equip is not an accredited educational institution. Whether any
particular school, employer or authority recognises it is their decision and
not something we can promise.
