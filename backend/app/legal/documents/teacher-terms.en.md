# Teacher & Contributor Agreement

**Version 1.0 · in force from 17 September 2026**

This agreement is for people who **teach** on Equip: teachers and directors. It
is in addition to the [Terms of Use](/terms) and the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy), which still apply to you; where this document says
something more specific, this document wins.

You are being asked to accept it because you can now do three things a student
cannot: put material in front of other people, see other people's work, and
decide what a mark and a certificate say. Each of those carries a
responsibility, and they are set out below in plain words so that nobody has to
guess where the line is.

## 1. Material you put into a course

**You confirm, for every single file and every single page you upload or write,
that you have the right to put it there.** Not "probably". Not "everybody uses
it". You confirm that the material is one of these:

- **yours** — you wrote it, made it, photographed it, recorded it; or
- **licensed to you** for this use, and you can show the licence; or
- **in the public domain**, or published under a licence that permits this use
  (Creative Commons and similar), and you have kept the attribution the licence
  requires; or
- **used within a genuine exception in the law that applies** — a short quotation
  with a citation, for instance.

**What this rules out, explicitly.** Uploading any of the following is a breach
of this agreement by you:

- a **scanned or photographed book**, or pages of one, that you do not hold the
  rights to;
- a **PDF or e-book taken from a file-sharing or "free library" site** —
  including sites that present themselves as Christian libraries. That a file
  was free to download says nothing about whether it was lawful to publish, and
  it is your responsibility to know the difference;
- a **translation** of a work you do not hold the translation rights to;
- **study guides, curricula, slide decks or handouts belonging to another
  school, seminary or publisher**, uploaded without their permission;
- **sheet music, hymn texts, images, maps, diagrams or photographs** taken from
  the web because they were findable;
- a **photograph or recording of a person** who did not agree to be shown, and
  of a child without the permission of whoever is responsible for them;
- anything you are under a **confidentiality obligation** about — from an
  employer, another school, or a publisher.

If you are not sure, do not upload it. Link to where it is lawfully published
instead, or ask us at supportequip@gmail.com.

**What follows from a breach.** Material uploaded without the right to do so is
**your** breach, not the platform's. We will remove it when we learn of it, we
will tell you, and if a claim is made against us because of it, section 6
applies. Repeated breaches cost you the teaching role and, if they continue,
the account.

**The licence you grant.** The licence you give Equip over material you upload
is set out in the Terms of Use, section "Material you upload" — non-exclusive,
worldwide, royalty-free, for storing, displaying, caching, reformatting and
machine-translating the material, surviving deletion only as far as already
issued certificates, grade sheets and records require. Nothing here widens it,
and nothing here takes your ownership away.

**Machine translation.** Course content you write is sent to a machine
translation provider so the platform can teach in four languages. By putting
material into a course you agree to that happening to it, and you confirm that
nothing in it is confidential or barred from being processed that way.

## 2. What you teach

Equip serves students from Reformed, Evangelical, Pentecostal, Catholic,
Orthodox and other backgrounds. The platform does not adjudicate between
traditions, and inside your own course you teach your own convictions — that is
what a school is for.

What you take responsibility for:

- **The doctrinal content of your lessons is yours.** Equip does not review it,
  does not endorse it, and is not answerable for it. If a student asks who
  taught something, the answer is you and your school.
- **Teach your own tradition; do not attack another.** Material whose purpose is
  to demean another Christian tradition, or any group of people, does not belong
  here. Neither does anything presenting a contested doctrine as the platform's
  position rather than your school's.
- **No hatred, no harassment, nothing unlawful, nothing that endangers a
  student.**
- **Say where something came from.** Quote and cite. A lesson assembled out of
  unattributed material from elsewhere is a problem whether or not it is also a
  copyright problem.

Equip may remove material, or your ability to publish it, at its discretion —
see section 5.

## 3. Student data you can see

Teaching gives you access to other people's work, marks, progress, contact
details and, in some places, their photographs. That access is given to you for
one purpose: teaching the people in your course.

You agree to:

- **use it only for teaching** the students it belongs to;
- **not export, copy, publish or pass it on** — no sharing a grade sheet outside
  the school, no posting a student's essay anywhere, no adding student addresses
  to a mailing list, no sending student data to an AI tool or any other service
  that is not part of this platform;
- **not discuss a student's work or marks** with anybody who has no business
  knowing;
- **take extra care with students under 18** — everything above, and no private
  contact outside the platform beyond what your school's own safeguarding rules
  allow;
- **tell us promptly at supportequip@gmail.com** if student data has gone
  somewhere it should not have, including if the cause was your own device or
  account.

When you stop teaching a course, your access to that course's students ends.
Anything you had copied elsewhere in the meantime you delete.

## 4. Marks, feedback and certificates

A mark and a certificate are the most consequential things this platform
produces. Somebody will show them to somebody else.

You agree to:

- **mark honestly and on the work** — against the course's own criteria, not
  against who the student is, where they are from, what they believe or what
  they can pay;
- **not award a mark, a completion or a certificate that was not earned**, and
  not withhold one that was;
- **mark the work in front of you** — you may use tools to help you write
  feedback, but the judgement is yours and you stand behind it;
- **give feedback a person can act on**, in reasonable time;
- **apply the course's own AI rule as written**, including the platform's
  position that an honestly declared use of AI is a declaration and not a
  forgery, and that we do not run submissions through AI detectors;
- **correct a mark you got wrong**, rather than leave it because it has been
  entered.

Every mark, change of mark and certificate decision is recorded against the
person who made it, with the time. That record is deliberate: a signature
matters because it can be traced.

## 5. What the platform may do

**Equip may remove any material, revoke the teaching role, and suspend or close
any account, at its discretion and without notice.** We are not obliged to
publish anything, and we do not become a publisher of what you upload by
choosing to leave it up.

We will do it when material or conduct breaks this agreement, when somebody
credibly says it does, when it puts the platform or its users at legal risk,
when it endangers somebody, or when it crosses the line in section 2. Where it
is practical we will say why, and where we get it wrong we will put it right —
but neither is a condition of acting.

Losing the teaching role does not delete what you built: material you uploaded
for a school's course stays available to that school and its students under the
licence in the Terms of Use, until the school removes it.

## 6. If your material causes a claim

If somebody brings a claim against Equip, or against the people behind it,
because of material you uploaded or wrote, because of how you handled student
data, or because you broke this agreement — **you will defend us against that
claim and cover what it costs us**, including reasonable legal fees and any
settlement or award. We will tell you about the claim promptly, let you run the
defence if you want it, and not settle it in your name without asking you.

This is the same indemnity as in the Terms of Use, and it is repeated here
because it is the clause most likely to actually matter to a teacher.

## 7. You are not our employee

Teaching on Equip creates no employment, no partnership and no agency. Nobody is
paid by Equip for teaching here, and nothing in this agreement entitles you to
payment. Your relationship with the school you teach for is between you and the
school.

The **no warranties**, **limits of liability** and **disputes** sections of the

## 9. Changes to this agreement

The same rule as the other documents. **A material change** — one that adds an
obligation on you, widens the licence you grant, changes the dispute sections,
or changes what happens to your material when you stop teaching — **asks you to
accept it again**. Anything else is published with the date it changed and
notified to you inside the platform, without a fresh signature.

## 10. Languages

This agreement is published in English, Russian, German and Ukrainian. **If they
differ, the English version governs** — except where the law of the country you
live in says otherwise.
